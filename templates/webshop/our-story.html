ORIGINS

A small atelier with a long view

The house began with a simple idea: to create jewelry that feels personal, not seasonal. Pieces that sit comfortably on the skin, age beautifully and quietly become part of your daily ritual.

From the start, our team chose to work closely with a handful of goldsmiths and stone setters. Every ring, necklace and earring is handled by people who know their craft — not pushed through a large production line.

Today, the collection includes both classic silhouettes and contemporary lines, but the approach remains the same: honest materials, considered proportions and details you only notice when you hold the piece in your hand.

TODAY & TOMORROW

Pieces made to stay

Whether you discover the house through an engagement ring, a simple chain or a pair of earrings, the intention is the same: to offer something you will still enjoy wearing years from now.

We continue to refine our shapes, explore new materials – including lab-grown diamonds – and keep production close to people we trust. The goal isn’t to be everywhere, but to be meaningful wherever our pieces appear.
